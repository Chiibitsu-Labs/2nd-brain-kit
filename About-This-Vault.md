# About this vault

This is your **second brain** — a private home for your notes that Claude
and ChatGPT can read and write, and that remembers what your AI learns
about you between conversations.

Everything here is yours: the notes, the accounts it runs on, the keys
that protect it. You can download the whole vault anytime (GitHub → Code →
Download ZIP) and walk away with it.

## Where things live

- `daily/` — day-to-day notes
- `notes/` — everything else you or your AI files away
- `ai-improvements/` — what your AI has learned about working with you
- `00_moc/` — indexes ("maps of content") that tie it together
